About Red Lum Impex Pvt. Ltd.

We are a dynamic trading firm specializing in the distribution of conduit pipes, committed to quality, reliability, and innovation.

Our Mission

To streamline the supply chain of conduit piping by collaborating with trusted manufacturers and leveraging our logistics expertise to provide superior products.

Our Vision

To be the leading supplier of conduit pipes in the industry, recognized for our quality, reliability, and innovation.

Who We Are & What We Do

Red Lum Impex Pvt. Ltd. is a dynamic trading firm specializing in the distribution of conduit pipes. Our business model involves purchasing high-quality raw materials, sending them to our manufacturing partners who produce durable and efficient conduit pipes, and then distributing them through multiple market channels.
